Helpful answers

Frequently asked questions.

Know what to expect before bringing in your phone.

Are the prices shown on this website final?

No. They are sample starting prices for demonstration. The final quote depends on your exact phone model, part availability and inspection. Please confirm the price before approving a repair.

Do I need to book before visiting?

It is best to call or send a WhatsApp enquiry first so the shop can confirm availability and advise you before you visit.

Which phone brands can I enquire about?

You can enquire about Samsung, Apple, Vivo, Oppo, Realme, Redmi, Xiaomi, OnePlus, Motorola and other popular brands.

Will I be informed before a repair is started?

Ask for the diagnosis, final price, part details and any applicable warranty terms before approving the repair.
